## Aluminium pieces

9 pieces must be made from the [Aluminium plate]. The CAD files are located in the folder Aluplates.

## Cable chain holders

The cable chains are secured to the frame at 6 points:
- Y-chain: this one contains the cables to control the motion of the Y axis. It is secured to the 250mm aluminium profile poking out of the frame, and to the mobile Y-carriage via a dedicated holder secured to the Y-axis motor holder (Y cable chain holder).
- Z-chain 1: this is the first segment that leads to the Z carriage. It is connected to the upper part of the 250mm aluminium profile on one end, and on the mobile Y-carriage via another dedicated holder secured to the Aluminium plate 5 (Z cable chain 1 holder).
- Z-chain 2: that one starts from another holder located on Aluminium plate 5 (Z cable chain 2 holder Y) and runs to the Z-carriage via a holder placed on Aluminium plate 8 (Z cable chain 2 holder Z).

## Endstop detector holders

The holders for the 6 magnetic endstop detectors must be 3D printed. The STL files are located in 'Limit switch holder', in the mechanical system folder.

## Endstop plates

The endstop detectors rely on metallic plates to detect the end of their course. These also need to be made, but there are no drawings to date.
